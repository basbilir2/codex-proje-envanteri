# Current status — 23 October 2026

Design Recovery aims to understand an entire design inside a photo/mockup and prepare an image-specific reconstruction instruction. Preserving source lettering, figures, geometry, composition and colors while removing fabric, folds, shadow and perspective is the goal. Source-preserving extraction and AI reconstruction are distinct workflows; sharpening/upscaling are auxiliary.

The latest application and training scripts are included. Local installation of the newer understanding adapter, its integration into the production pipeline and general reconstruction quality remain unverified. Experimental training does not constitute product acceptance. Customer/reference artwork, training data, credentials, cloud account records and checkpoints remain private. No GPU resources are started by this repository.
